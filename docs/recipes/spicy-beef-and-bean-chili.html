Spicy Beef and Bean Chili

A pepper-packed chili with a fresh lime finish and optional beans, based on 1.5 lb of beef.

chilibeefspicy
01

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin.

02

Method

  1. Add 1 bulb garlic (diced small) and 1-2 onion (whole, red or yellow, or mixed; diced) to oil and simmer until it begins to steam.
  2. Add 10 jalapenos (chopped finely) and 10 habaneros (or serranos; chopped finely), and let it simmer for a bit.
  3. Add 10-15 roma tomatoes (chopped finely) and the spices: 1 tbsp cumin, 2 tsp paprika, about 3 tbsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp pepper flakes, 4-5 tbsp MSG, about 0.5 tbsp salt, and pepper to taste. Let it simmer until most of the excess water has boiled away.
  4. Reserve however much of the salsa you want before adding the meat.
  5. Add 1.5 lb ground beef and, if using, 1 can kidney beans (or pinto beans). Add 3 tbsp chilli powder. Mix well, breaking up the beef, and simmer for about 15 minutes, until the beef is fully cooked and the chili has reached the desired thickness.
  6. Finish with 4 limes (juiced), then serve.

✳ A note from the cook

This batch uses 1.5 lb of ground beef. Beans are optional; use kidney beans or pinto beans. Serranos may replace the habaneros. Reserve any salsa before adding the meat.
