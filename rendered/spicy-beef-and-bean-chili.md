# Spicy Beef and Bean Chili

#chili #beef #spicy

> A pepper-packed chili with a fresh lime finish and optional beans, based on 1.5 lb of beef.

## Ingredients

- *1 bulb* garlic (diced small)
- *1-2* onion (whole, red or yellow, or mixed; diced)
- oil
- *10* jalapenos (chopped finely)
- *10* habaneros (or serranos; chopped finely)
- *10-15* roma tomatoes (chopped finely)
- *1 tbsp* cumin
- *2 tsp* paprika
- *3 tbsp* garlic powder
- *2 tsp* onion powder
- *1 tsp* pepper flakes
- *4-5 tbsp* MSG
- *1/2 tbsp* salt
- pepper
- *1 1/2 lb* ground beef
- *1 can* kidney beans (optional) (or pinto beans)
- *3 tbsp* chilli powder
- *4* limes (juiced)

## Steps

> **Note:** This batch uses 1.5 lb of ground beef. Beans are optional; use kidney beans or pinto beans. Serranos may replace the habaneros. Reserve any salsa before adding the meat.

1. Add garlic and onion to oil and simmer until it begins to steam.

2. Add jalapenos and habaneros, and let it simmer for a bit.

3. Add roma tomatoes and the spices: cumin, paprika, about garlic powder, onion powder, pepper flakes, MSG, about salt, and pepper to taste. Let it simmer until most of the excess water has boiled away.

4. Reserve however much of the salsa you want before adding the meat.

5. Add ground beef and, if using, kidney beans. Add chilli powder. Mix well, breaking up the beef, and simmer for about 15 minutes, until the beef is fully cooked and the chili has reached the desired thickness.

6. Finish with limes, then serve.
